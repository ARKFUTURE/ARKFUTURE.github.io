EPILOGUE

We embrace the hacker ethos.

Hacker ethic is a term used to describe the passion for problem-solving and overcoming limitations.

"Anyone can be a hacker. You can be a carpenter hacker. It doesn't have to be high-tech. As long as it involves skill and you are deeply engaged in what you are doing, you can be a hacker."

— from The Hacker Ethic and the Spirit of the Information Age

We also uphold the GNU spirit, believing that openness fosters collective progress.

We advocate for lifelong learning—continuous accumulation enriches one's life, whether in or out of work.

We are a group of individuals pursuing our dreams.
