We embrace the hacker spirit.

The term "hacker" is used to describe people who are passionate about solving problems and overcoming limitations.

"Anyone can be a hacker. You could be a carpenter hacker. It does not have to be high-tech. As long as it involves skill, and you are devoted to what you are doing, you can be a hacker."

Quoted from The Hacker Ethic and the Spirit of the Information Age

We also uphold the GNU spirit, believing that openness allows us to make progress together.

We advocate lifelong learning—continuous accumulation enriches your life, whether you are working or not.

We are a group of people pursuing our own dreams.
